Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 22, 2026

Overview

PlutoAnalyst ("the App") is an investment analysis tool that displays publicly available market data for stocks, ETFs, and cryptocurrencies. We are committed to protecting your privacy.

Data Collection

PlutoAnalyst does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. Specifically:

  • No account registration or login is required
  • No personal information is collected (name, email, phone, etc.)
  • No location data is accessed or stored
  • No cookies or tracking technologies are used
  • No analytics or advertising SDKs are integrated
  • No data is shared with third parties

Market Data

The App fetches publicly available financial market data from third-party sources (Yahoo Finance) to display rankings and metrics. This data is cached temporarily on our servers for performance purposes and does not contain any user-specific information.

Device Permissions

PlutoAnalyst requires only internet access to fetch market data. No other device permissions (camera, microphone, contacts, storage, etc.) are requested.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect any information from children.

Disclaimer

The information provided by PlutoAnalyst is for educational and informational purposes only. It does not constitute financial advice, investment recommendations, or an endorsement of any security. Always conduct your own research and consult a qualified financial advisor before making investment decisions.
